# Case Initiation and Ground-Truth Protocol — LIBERA

## 1. Purpose

This document fixes the investigative starting point **before** any forensic examiner, baseline investigator, or AI system reviews the acquired WhatsApp evidence.

It prevents hindsight bias and circular reasoning.

The scenario is a fictionalized Indonesian adaptation structurally derived from the public Galloway record. It is not presented as a real Indonesian prosecution.

## 2. Separation of three things

### A. Pre-device investigative basis
Information investigators have before examining Raka's phone.

### B. Device evidence
What is later recovered from the test handset and may support, weaken, or complicate the investigative hypothesis.

### C. Evaluator-only ground truth
What the research team knows because the scenario was constructed from the adjudicated Galloway case. This is hidden from examiner/AI until P9 evaluation.

## 3. Why Raka is initially suspected

The initiating suspicion must not depend on the same WhatsApp messages that will later be evaluated.

The fictional Raka case is therefore initiated from **non-device / pre-examination information adapted from documented Galloway case facts**, including:

1. A victim/participant analogue has left the organization and reports coercive/control behavior.
2. An emergency-call incident records the suspect attempting to compel the person to return and warning against contacting police.
3. Investigators have information linking the suspected network to repeated hotel/lodging use associated with commercial-sex activity.
4. Witness/victim information describes a coordinated organization in which appointments, movement, lodging, money, and participant availability are controlled by the central organizer.
5. The investigation already concerns suspected commercial sex trafficking; the handset is sought as a likely repository of communications relevant to recruitment, appointments, movement, control, finances, and co-actor coordination.

These points establish an **investigative hypothesis and examination scope**, not guilt.

## 4. Simulated legal/procedural wrapper

The public materials used for LIBERA do not establish, with sufficient detail for this project, the exact historical procedure by which Cornelius Galloway's particular handset was seized and searched.

Therefore LIBERA must not invent that procedure and attribute it to the real case.

For the academic simulation, use the following explicit wrapper:

> A researcher-owned test handset, designated DEV-001, is configured to represent a handset lawfully submitted to a digital-forensic laboratory in a trafficking investigation. The examination is performed under simulated search authority for research/training purposes.

The report must state that this is a simulation.

Do not write that law enforcement actually seized the user's personal phone.

## 5. Physical host for the simulation

Preferred:
- dedicated spare Android handset owned by a team member; or
- a dedicated test handset/account.

Acceptable with caution:
- the user's own handset as the **physical host**, only if a dedicated test environment/account can be separated from real personal data.

In the paper, the device should be identified only as:
- `DEV-001`;
- make/model;
- OS/build;
- app version;
- acquisition method.

It should **not** be described as Christian's personal phone in the case narrative.

The correct wording is:

> DEV-001 was a researcher-owned test device configured to simulate Raka's WhatsApp handset.

This preserves realism without falsely claiming a real seizure.

## 6. Investigative request to the examiner

The examiner receives a request similar to:

> Examine DEV-001 for WhatsApp and related digital artifacts relevant to a suspected human-trafficking investigation. Identify communications that may evidence recruitment, transport or movement, lodging, appointment coordination, money or pricing, coercion/control, attempts to leave or seek help, and coordination among actors. Reconstruct relevant timelines and relationships. Report both supporting and contradictory/insufficient evidence. Do not determine legal guilt.

The examiner does not receive:
- the Galloway court record;
- the synthetic provenance labels;
- the hidden relevant/distractor labels;
- the expected findings;
- the case-resolution ground truth.

## 7. Evaluator-only case ground truth

The Galloway outcome is used to define the intended scenario resolution.

Official public outcome:
- Cornelius Galloway pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit sex trafficking.
- He admitted participation in a ring using force, threats, fraud and coercion.
- He admitted that the organization advertised commercial sex online, rented hotel rooms, arranged appointments by phone, controlled hours/fees/locations, and took the proceeds.
- He was later sentenced to 17 years in federal prison.

For LIBERA, the fictional analogue is:

> **Raka is the intended central offender in the synthetic scenario ground truth, corresponding structurally to Cornelius Galloway's admitted role.**

This statement belongs to the evaluator package and post-analysis research discussion, not the blinded examiner brief.

## 8. Why the forensic report should not simply say "Raka is guilty"

Digital forensic examination establishes and interprets digital evidence. It does not independently adjudicate guilt.

The examiner may conclude, for example:

- evidence strongly supports central coordination of appointments;
- evidence supports control over movement/lodging;
- evidence supports financial control;
- evidence supports coercive or monitoring behavior;
- evidence is consistent with a trafficking scheme;
- evidence is insufficient for a particular allegation.

The **research evaluator**, after unlocking ground truth, then asks whether the examiner's findings are consistent with the intended case resolution.

## 9. Final two-layer conclusion

### Layer 1 — Blinded forensic conclusion
What can be supported from acquired evidence alone.

### Layer 2 — Research ground-truth comparison
Whether those findings correctly recover the synthetic case facts adapted from the adjudicated Galloway outcome.

A strong final result would therefore read conceptually:

> The forensic examination recovered multiple independent message clusters supporting recruitment/appointment coordination, lodging and movement logistics, financial control, and coercive monitoring by Raka. These findings were traceable to acquired artifacts and were broadly concordant with the evaluator-only ground truth adapted from the adjudicated Galloway case.

This is stronger and more defensible than:

> The phone proves Raka is guilty.
